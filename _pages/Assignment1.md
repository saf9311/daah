What is science fiction truly about? At first, we believed that science fiction was similar to fantasy novels, but by understanding it in depth, we now realize that it’s mainly only about space and technological advances, and how they influence human society. Moreover, our hypothesis is that we assume that space and time will be brought up a lot; not only that, but we believe that time periods will significantly affect the commonalities in science fiction books.

To begin with, we noticed that ‘war’ was the most common word out of everything, especially when it was about society. It was mentioned 9234 times which was the only blue box compared to every other word, which indicates it being the only word found over 7500 times. This highlights how a lot of science fiction books contain mentions of war, as hardships and battles build up on a story a lot, as well as make it more interesting. Also, another reason war is mentioned a lot is because of the era many of the science fiction books were written, as many were during the World Wars or the cold war which heavily impacted the thought process in writing these books. There were many system collapses, and power struggles between sovereigns during that era, which caused many wars to happen as economies fell, and rulers fought (caused fatal outbreaks). The authors also wrote while being influenced by the heavy technological advances of military machinery like: airplanes, chemical warfare, and the atomic bomb. We never expected that war would be brought up a plethora of times, as we initially thought space and time were going to be the most found words in every story.

![wordsbytag](../assets/images/wordbytag.png)

Secondly, as we analyzed the patterns, we realized that spaceship is the least relevant word in all the texts, even the one about space travel. The usual stereotype that follows sci-fi is that space travel will be very significant in all its stories, however society, political drama, and a wide mix of dystopian themes are more frequent. That is because writers of that era were far more interested in human life and how a society can survive during hard times, hence war being mentioned a lot due to it being a way people protect themselves when it comes to any enemy. To add on, the color of its box was white for three texts which were: society, war, and action, demonstrating that it was 0.03 (lower than 0.25 of being relevant). The distinction matters here as one would expect texts that fall under the category of science fiction would always have spaceships being significant to the story, but it is evident now that this is not the case.
